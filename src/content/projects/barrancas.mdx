## The problem

Barrancas came about to digitize and consolidate a set of processes that already existed in educational institutions but were done manually and on paper. Information about students, wellbeing and warning signs was being collected, but the process was slow, fragmented and hard to scale.

That affected both efficiency and reach. Consolidating results took time, following cases required manual work, and the information wasn't always clear enough to act on quickly. The problem wasn't just collecting data, but organizing it in a way that made it possible to detect students at risk and follow up with better judgment and less operational friction.

## The solution

The platform was designed to bring that entire flow into a single system. It wasn't about simply moving forms onto a screen, but about digitizing an existing institutional process and making it more efficient, more traceable and easier to sustain over time.

Barrancas made it possible to configure surveys for each institution's context, centralize responses, identify risk signals and give visibility to the information through dashboards and reports. That reduced the scattering of the process and made it easier for leadership teams and teachers to understand their students' situation and act earlier.

## My role

I was responsible for the platform's full-stack development, from the backend architecture to the interface implementation. I also took part in the product's continuous evolution based on real use and feedback from the educational teams.

An important part of the work was translating existing processes, with their own logic and limitations, into a digital experience that didn't add unnecessary complexity. The focus wasn't only on building features, but on making the system clear, operable and useful in contexts where time and capacity for follow-up are limited.

## Technical decisions

Backend with AdonisJS to keep a clear organization as the product grew. Frontend with Edge + Vue: server-side rendering for stability, and interactivity where it was needed without oversizing.

On that foundation: configurable surveys, visualizations for teams, automated reports and case follow-up. The key decision wasn't the stack, but how to structure things to grow without losing clarity.

## Product evolution

We're currently working on a new follow-up and support module. The idea: not to stop at detection, but to also support the continuity of the intervention.
