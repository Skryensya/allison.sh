## The problem

Barrancas came about to digitize and consolidate processes that already existed in educational institutions but were done by hand and on paper. Information about students, wellbeing and warning signs was being collected, but the process was slow, fragmented and hard to scale.

That affected both efficiency and reach. Consolidating results took time, following a case required manual work, and the information wasn't always clear enough to act on quickly. Collecting data was the easy part. The hard part was organizing it so students at risk could be detected in time and followed up with better judgment and less operational friction.

## The solution

The platform brings that entire flow into a single system. Digitizing an existing institutional process was much more than moving forms onto a screen: it had to become more efficient, more traceable and easier to sustain over time.

Barrancas lets you configure surveys for each institution's context, centralize responses, identify risk signals and show the information in dashboards and reports. That reduces the scattering of the process, and leadership teams and teachers understand their students' situation better and can act earlier.

## My role

I was responsible for the platform's full-stack development, from the backend architecture to the interface, and I took part in its continuous evolution based on real use and feedback from the educational teams.

A good part of the work was translating existing processes, with their own logic and limitations, into a digital experience that didn't add unnecessary complexity. More than building features, what mattered was that the system be clear, operable and useful where time and capacity for follow-up are limited.

## Technical decisions

Backend with AdonisJS, to keep a clear organization as the product grew. Frontend with Edge + Vue: server-side rendering for stability, and interactivity only where it was needed, without oversizing.

On that foundation: configurable surveys, visualizations for teams, automated reports and case follow-up. The key decision wasn't the stack but how to structure the system to grow without losing clarity.

## Product evolution

We're working on a new follow-up and support module. The idea is not to stop at detection but to also support the continuity of the intervention.
