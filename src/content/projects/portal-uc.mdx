## The problem

The Portal UC is the university's digital front door: news, academic information and essential services for more than 33,000 students, 3,500 faculty members and thousands of staff.

Being that entry point means every improvement has a real impact. But it also means working on a site that is constantly evolving and under heavy demand.

## My role

I work in three areas: new sections and redesigns, operational maintenance and accessibility. Everything is coordinated with the Kit Digital UC so that changes benefit the rest of the institutional web ecosystem.

## Technical decisions

The Portal uses the Kit Digital UC as the base of components shared with the ecosystem, but it has its own set in Vue for more complex behavior. Its own components keep the same visual style, differing where the Portal, as the main site, requires it.

Stack: PHP with an existing CMS, CSS/SCSS, Vanilla JS and Vue.

## The hard part

The real challenge isn't technical. It's bureaucratic.

Working with an institutional client means navigating approvals, crossed priorities and processes that aren't designed for quick changes. Improvements that in another context would take days can stretch into weeks because they need validation at different levels.

I move in small incremental steps, not for lack of ability, but because that's the pace the context allows. I learned to celebrate small wins and to measure progress not in speed but in sustained impact.
