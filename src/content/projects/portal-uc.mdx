## The problem

The Portal UC is the university's digital front door: news, academic information and essential services for more than 33,000 students, 3,500 faculty members and thousands of staff.

Being that entry point means every improvement has a real impact. It also means working on a site that never stops evolving and is under heavy demand.

## My role

I work in three areas: new sections and redesigns, operational maintenance and accessibility. Everything is coordinated with the Kit Digital UC so that changes also benefit the rest of the institutional web ecosystem.

## Technical decisions

The Portal uses the Kit Digital UC as the base of components shared with the ecosystem, but it has its own set in Vue for the more complex behavior. Those components keep the same visual style and differ where the Portal, as the main site, needs it.

<ImageFigure src={portalUcNoticia} alt="A news story on uc.cl: “UC lidera tecnología chilena para mejorar la precisión en el estudio del origen del Universo”, with its lead, date and an information panel with unit and strategic axis." caption="A Portal news story: headline, lead and information panel" />

Stack: PHP with an existing CMS, CSS/SCSS, Vanilla JS and Vue.

## The hard part

The real challenge isn't technical, it's bureaucratic.

Working with an institutional client means navigating approvals, crossed priorities and processes that aren't designed for quick changes. Improvements that in another context would take days can take weeks because they need validation at several levels.

I move in small steps, not for lack of ability but because that's the pace the context allows. I learned to celebrate small wins and to measure progress by sustained impact, not by speed.
